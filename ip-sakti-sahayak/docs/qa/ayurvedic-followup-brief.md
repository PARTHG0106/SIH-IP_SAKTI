**Step-by-step strategy**

1. Inference/application — Preserve confidentiality and decide what to disclose: Limit access to non-public formulae and process parameters, use appropriate confidentiality agreements and document who receives the information. [trade_secret] Give the patent drafter the reproducible method, relevant best-method details, biological-material sources and geographical origins. [patents_specification]

2. Inference/application — Confirm the product category and licensing path: Compare the complete formula and intended medicinal use with the First-Schedule books before choosing a licensing category. [dc_3a] Prepare an ingredient-by-ingredient list of authoritative-book references and record the administration route for regulatory review. [dc_3h]

3. Inference/application — Resolve material sourcing and access obligations: Record incorporation, ownership/control and relevant residency facts, then assess s.3 before the proposed access. [bda_s3] Create a material-by-material sourcing record: identity, source/location, supplier, wild or cultivated status, access date and intended use. [bda_scope]

4. Inference/application — Assess the proposed patent claims: Document the claimed technical contribution and commission a prior-art and patentability assessment before selecting claims. [patents_invention] Assess patentability and freedom to operate separately, and coordinate any patent application with the Patent Office route. [patents_rights]
