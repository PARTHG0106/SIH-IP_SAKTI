# Source audit and additions — 27 September 2026

The corpus contains **54 records: 33 `primary_checked`, 19 `dossier_record`, and 2 `needs_review`**. The 33 checked records comprise 31 statutory/regulatory summaries and 2 official explanatory references (the trade-secret overview and Copyright Office handbook). `primary_checked` means the cited passage was checked in an identified source; it does **not** mean every subsequent amendment, notification, judgment, local practice or application-specific fact has been verified.

`corpus/primary/manifest.json` inventories **16 valid PDFs**, with titles, source URLs, SHA-256 digests, page counts, review scope and linked record IDs. All 33 checked records map to an archived document. Fifteen archives match a fresh download from the identified official publisher or WIPO Lex; one 2024 CDSCO compilation matches the pre-existing file supplied in the parent workspace. No failed download was identified and no source file was deleted.

## Provenance and date limits

- WIPO Lex hosts the statutory PDFs used here. The landing pages identify the relevant English documents. Their unsigned resource URLs redirect to HTML; the signed PDF links on those pages yielded bytes matching every archived WIPO PDF. The manifest keeps stable landing/resource URLs, not expiring query signatures.
- The Indian statutes hosted by WIPO Lex are primary statutory texts. The WIPO trade-secret overview is secondary explanatory material from an official intergovernmental source, and is explicitly tagged `official_reference`; do not present its commentary as statutory language.
- The 2016 CDSCO compilation was freshly matched to its official URL. `drugs-rules-2024-supplied.pdf` is a supplementary cross-check, with a digest matching `../Drugs Rules 1945_2024 09.pdf` relative to the project root. Its exact original download URL was not recovered. It identifies CDSCO as publisher and states amendment through 1 July 2024. The corpus does not assert a complete drug-law consolidation as of September 2026.
- The [NBA-hosted corrigenda](https://www.nbaindia.nic.in/sites/default/files/2026-05/Corrigendum_1.pdf) were also inspected online. Corrections to the section 6/7 pages are spelling corrections; they do not change the distinctions used here. This supplementary file was not added to the archive or given a new corpus record.
- `as_of` is a source/provision date and may be an original Act date, amendment date, compilation date or, for the undated copyright handbook, retrieval date as expressly noted in its review note. It must not be rendered as proof that the whole law was consolidated on that date. `reviewed_on` records passage review; it does not certify legal currency.
- The archive's `.pdf.txt` files are extraction aids. The PDFs, not OCR or extracted text alone, are the source of record. The rule 158B tables were checked by complete relevant pages, including row/column context.

Rates or exemptions must not be assumed from the herb name or final product alone.

The source-supported application should explain these concrete limits rather than substitute a blanket disclaimer for an answer.
